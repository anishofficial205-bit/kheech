YOUR OWN VIDEOS
  1. 01Click CHOOSE VIDEOS below, or drag video files anywhere onto this screen. Add as many as you like.
  2. 02Make your frame. The first video plays inside it, and pauses when you close the frame.
  3. 03Close and reopen the frame, or pinch one hand, to move to the next video. Drag the video chips in the bottom bar to change the order.
  4. 04VIDEO FIT on the right: BEHIND fills the screen behind your frame, INSIDE fits the video into the frame. Turn on VIDEO AUDIO for sound.

Your videos stay on this computer and are cleared when you refresh the page.
