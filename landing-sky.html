Your webcam watches just your thumbs and index fingers. Wherever they point, the picture changes.

It runs entirely in your browser. A small hand-tracking model finds 21 points on each hand, about thirty times a second. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Close the frame and open it again to get the next look, or drop in your own videos.
