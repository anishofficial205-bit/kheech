How it worksFiltersYour videosPrivacy

Your webcam watches just your thumbs and index fingers. Wherever they point, the picture changes.

  1. STEP 01

    Make an L

    Thumb out, index finger up, on both hands. Bring the corners together.

  2. STEP 02

    Pull it apart

    Spread your hands. The gap between your fingers becomes a window.

  3. STEP 03

    Close, reopen

    Pinch the frame shut and open it again. Every round brings a new look.

SHORTCUTS

Hand tricks

  • PINCH

    While the frame is open, pinch one hand to skip to the next look.

  • PEACE SIGN

    Hold it up for a 3-2-1 countdown and a photo.

  • PULL WIDER

    The further apart your hands, the stronger the look gets.

YOUR OWN VIDEOS

Play any video through your fingers

  1. 01In the camera, press + VIDEO or drag video files onto the screen. Add as many as you like.
  2. 02Make your frame. Your video plays inside it, and pauses when you close the frame.
  3. 03Close and reopen the frame, or pinch, for the next video. Drag the video chips to change the order.

Pick BEHIND to reveal the video behind your frame, or INSIDE to fit it into the frame. Turn on video audio for sound. Your videos never leave your computer.

Runs entirely in your browser. A small hand-tracking model finds 21 points on each hand, about thirty times a second. Nothing is uploaded. Choose a rectangle or circle frame, take photos, and record clips of what you make.
